# From workshop to production

Prototypes and reusable examples may remain here with a named maintainer. A production app graduates to its own repository, operator/support contact, YouVersion Platform registration and licensed app access, credentials, telemetry, deployment and rollback runbook. Review current Platform Go Live requirements and quotas before traffic. Workshop approval does not authorize use of another app's key or deployment.

The maintainer records the new repository and status in project.json and README, and updates the mission effort record. Preserve provenance and contributor credit when moving files. Leave a small migration notice here; do not silently redirect users or delete history. Retire abandoned prototypes explicitly. This workshop has no production hosting, common app keys, shared end-user database or deployment pipeline.
